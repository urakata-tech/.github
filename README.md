# urakat.tech

**Tools for the people behind the scenes.**
裏方 (urakata) means the crew backstage: the people who make the show happen.

urakata. is a small app studio in Tokyo. We build software for people who run things: events, communities and small businesses. Each product starts from a problem we've had ourselves.

## Kido by urakata
**[kido.urakata.jp](https://kido.urakata.jp)**

Kido (木戸) is the gate of a traditional Japanese theatre, where guests paid their admission (木戸銭). Kido is the back office for independent event organisers.

Ticketing apps sell tickets. Kido handles everything after that:

- **Guest lists**: DJs and promoters fill in their own list through a shared link
- **The door**: walk-ins, cash and PayPay, live headcount on every phone
- **Final import**: bring in your Luma, Peatix and Meetup lists after the event to see who came
- **Money**: income, expenses and who owes whom between partners
- **Guest history**: one place to see who comes back, across every platform and event

Built in Tokyo, tested at real events. English and 日本語.

> Status: private pilot. Want to try it for your events? Get in touch.
